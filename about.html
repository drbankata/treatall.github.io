About TreatAll

Redefining elderly care with compassion, expertise, and innovation

Our Mission

To provide professional, compassionate elderly care that enables seniors to live with dignity, comfort, and independence in the familiar surroundings of their own homes.

We believe every elderly person deserves care that goes beyond basic assistance. Our mission is to deliver medical-grade, personalized care that addresses not just physical needs, but emotional well-being, mental stimulation, and social connection. Through our NSDC-certified attendants and technology-enabled monitoring, we bring hospital-quality care to the comfort of home.

TreatAll was founded on the principle that families shouldn't have to choose between professional healthcare and the warmth of home. We bridge that gap, giving families peace of mind while ensuring their loved ones receive the best possible care.

Our Story

TreatAll was born from a personal need that became a professional calling.

Like many families in India, we faced the challenge of finding quality care for our elderly loved ones. The options were limited: either move them to an institutional setting, losing the comfort of home, or hire untrained helpers who couldn't provide the medical attention required. Neither option felt right.

We realized that thousands of families across Delhi NCR faced the same dilemma. Professional healthcare shouldn't end at the hospital door. Seniors deserve access to trained medical attendants who can provide hospital-quality care while preserving the dignity, comfort, and familiarity of home.

That's when TreatAll was founded. We set out to create a service that combines professional medical training with genuine compassion, supported by technology that keeps families connected and informed. Today, we're proud to serve over 100 families across NCR, delivering more than 5,000 hours of exceptional care.

Every family we serve reminds us why we started: because quality elderly care isn't a luxury—it's a fundamental need that every senior and their family deserves.

Certifications & Partnerships

Recognized credentials and trusted healthcare partnerships

NSDC Certification

All our attendants are certified by the National Skill Development Corporation in Healthcare & Allied Services. This ensures they meet national standards for medical care, patient safety, and professional conduct.

Police Verification

Every single attendant undergoes thorough police background verification. We only hire individuals with clean records and verified identities, giving you complete peace of mind about who enters your home.

Healthcare Partners

We maintain partnerships with leading hospitals and clinics across NCR for emergency situations, specialist referrals, and coordinated care. Your loved one benefits from our established medical network.

IGNOU Training

Our training programs are developed in collaboration with IGNOU's School of Health Sciences, ensuring our curriculum reflects the latest in geriatric care best practices and medical protocols.

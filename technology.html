Technology-Enabled Care

Transparency and peace of mind through our family mobile app

Family Mobile App

Stay connected to your loved one's care 24/7 from anywhere in the world

Real-Time Vitals Dashboard

View blood pressure, blood sugar, oxygen levels, temperature, and pulse updated throughout the day. See trends over time with easy-to-read charts.

Daily Activity Log

Complete timeline of care activities with timestamps: meals eaten, medications taken, exercises completed, bathroom visits, and social activities.

Medication Tracking

View complete medication schedule. Get confirmation when each dose is administered. Never miss a medication with automated reminders.

Instant Alerts

Receive immediate notifications for any health concerns, missed medications, or changes in condition. Configure custom alert thresholds.

Photo & Video Updates

Attendants share photos and short videos throughout the day so you can see your loved one is comfortable and well-cared for.

Direct Messaging

Chat directly with attendant and supervisor. Ask questions, give instructions, share updates - all in one place.

Health Reports

Generate comprehensive health reports for doctor visits. Export data as PDF to share with healthcare providers.

Emergency Button

One-tap emergency call connects you directly to our 24/7 helpline. Supervisor alerted immediately with your loved one's current health data.

Data Security & Privacy

HIPAA-Equivalent Standards

All health data encrypted in transit and at rest. We follow international healthcare data protection standards.

Secure Access Controls

Only authorized family members can access the app. You control who sees what information.

Data Ownership

Your data belongs to you. Download or delete it anytime. No data sharing with third parties ever.

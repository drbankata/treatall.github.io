How TreatAll Works

From your first call to ongoing exceptional care in 6 simple steps

1
Same Day

FREE Consultation

Start your journey with a no-pressure conversation about your needs.

What Happens:

  • Call, WhatsApp, or fill our online form
  • Care coordinator calls you back within 2 hours
  • 20-minute friendly conversation about your situation
  • We understand your loved one's needs, medical conditions, and preferences
  • Get preliminary pricing and package recommendations
  • Schedule home assessment at your convenience

What You Need:

  • Basic information about your loved one's health status
  • Your availability for a home visit
  • Questions you want answered
No obligation. This consultation is completely free and there's zero pressure to commit.
2
24-48 Hours

Home Assessment

Our senior care coordinator visits your home for a comprehensive evaluation.

What Happens:

  • 60-90 minute in-home visit at a time convenient for you
  • Meet your loved one and observe their environment
  • Comprehensive health and mobility assessment
  • Discussion of daily routine, preferences, and medical needs
  • Home safety evaluation and recommendations
  • Q&A session with family members
  • Customized care plan development
  • Written proposal with transparent pricing delivered within 24 hours

What You Need:

  • Medical records and current medication list
  • Doctor's contact information
  • Family members present for discussion
  • Your loved one's daily schedule and preferences
3
48 Hours

Attendant Matching

We find the perfect fit for your family's unique needs and preferences.

What Happens:

  • We match you with 2-3 qualified candidates
  • Review detailed profiles including experience, certifications, and specializations
  • See real reviews from other families they've worked with
  • Meet candidates via video call or in-person
  • Your loved one can interact with them to assess comfort level
  • Choose the attendant who feels like the right fit
  • Background verification confirmation shared with you

Selection Criteria:

  • Medical expertise required for your loved one's conditions
  • Language preferences
  • Personality compatibility
  • Experience with similar cases
  • Shift timings and availability
4
Day 1

Onboarding & Training

Smooth transition with comprehensive setup and orientation on the first day.

What Happens:

  • Supervisor accompanies attendant on first day
  • Complete home orientation (kitchen, medical supplies, emergency exits)
  • Review of care plan with family and attendant
  • Medical protocols and emergency procedures established
  • Family app setup and training for real-time updates
  • Introduction to doctor, pharmacist, and other key contacts
  • Service agreement signing with clear terms
  • Emergency contact list creation
  • First day of supervised care begins

You'll Receive:

  • Family app login credentials
  • 24/7 emergency helpline number
  • Supervisor's direct contact
  • Care plan document
  • Service agreement copy
5
Ongoing

Care Begins

Personalized, professional care delivered daily with complete transparency.

Daily Care Includes:

  • Follow personalized care plan developed for your loved one
  • Real-time vitals monitoring and logging in family app
  • Medication administration per doctor's instructions
  • Daily activity reports with photos and updates
  • Mobility assistance and exercise routines
  • Meal preparation following dietary guidelines
  • Personal care and hygiene assistance
  • Companionship and mental stimulation activities

Family App Features:

  • View real-time health metrics (BP, sugar, temperature)
  • See daily activity log with timestamps
  • Track medication schedule and compliance
  • Receive instant alerts for any concerns
  • Message directly with attendant and supervisor
  • Access health reports for doctor visits
  • View photos and updates throughout the day
6
Continuous

Quality Assurance

Ongoing monitoring and improvement to ensure consistently excellent care.

Regular Check-ins:

  • Weekly Supervisor Visits: Physical inspection, quality checks, attendant performance review
  • Monthly Family Meetings: Discuss progress, address concerns, adjust care plan as needed
  • Quarterly Comprehensive Reviews: Full health assessment, care plan update, long-term planning

Quality Controls:

  • Random quality audits and surprise visits
  • Family satisfaction surveys
  • Attendant performance evaluations
  • Continuous training and skill upgrades
  • Medical protocol compliance checks

If Issues Arise:

  • Immediate concern resolution through 24/7 helpline
  • Attendant replacement within 48 hours if needed
  • Care plan modifications at any time
  • Package upgrades/downgrades based on changing needs
  • Easy cancellation with 15-day notice

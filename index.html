How TreatAll Works

Simple 6-step process from inquiry to exceptional care

1

FREE Consultation (Same Day)

Call, WhatsApp, or fill our form. Speak with a care coordinator within 2 hours. 20-minute needs assessment. No pressure, no obligation.

2

Home Assessment (Within 24-48 Hours)

Senior care coordinator visits your home. Meets your loved one. Comprehensive health and needs evaluation. Customized care plan created. Written proposal with transparent pricing.

3

Attendant Matching (48 Hours)

We match you with 2-3 potential attendants. Review profiles, experience, certifications. Meet them via video or in-person. Choose the best fit for your family.

4

Onboarding & Training (Day 1)

Supervisor present on first day. Home orientation and protocols review. Family app setup and training. Emergency procedures established. Service agreement signed.

5

Care Begins (Day 1 Onwards)

Attendant follows personalized care plan. Real-time updates via family app. Daily vitals tracked and logged. Medication schedules maintained. Regular supervisor check-ins.

6

Continuous Quality Assurance

Weekly supervisor visits. Monthly family review meetings. Quarterly care plan updates. 24/7 emergency helpline. Instant attendant replacement if needed (within 48 hours).

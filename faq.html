Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly can service start?▼
Service can typically begin within 3-5 days after your free home assessment. In urgent situations, we can often accommodate same-day or next-day starts.
Are all attendants background verified?▼
Yes, 100%. Every attendant undergoes thorough police verification, health screening, NSDC certification verification, and reference checks before joining TreatAll.
What if we're not satisfied with the attendant?▼
We guarantee a replacement within 48 hours, no questions asked. There are no additional fees for replacements. Your satisfaction is our priority.
Do you provide medical equipment?▼
We can arrange rental of hospital beds, wheelchairs, oxygen concentrators, and other equipment through our partner network. This is available as an add-on service.
What's included in medical monitoring?▼
Medical monitoring includes daily vitals tracking (BP, sugar, oxygen, temperature), medication administration, health alerts, digital reports, and coordination with doctors.
Can we change service levels?▼
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade packages at any time based on changing needs. We'll adjust your care plan accordingly with no penalties.
What's the cancellation policy?▼
No long-term contracts required. Cancel anytime with 15 days' notice. No cancellation fees for monthly plans. Annual plans have pro-rated refunds.
Do you work with insurance?▼
We provide detailed invoices and medical documentation that you can submit to your insurance for reimbursement. We can assist with paperwork but don't bill insurance directly.
What happens in medical emergencies?▼
Our 24/7 emergency helpline connects you to senior care coordinators. We have protocols with partner hospitals and average response time under 15 minutes. Attendants are trained in emergency procedures.

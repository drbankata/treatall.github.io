Our Services & Pricing

Comprehensive elderly care solutions tailored to every need

Choose the Right Level of Care

Every family's needs are unique. Our five specialized packages ensure you get exactly the right level of support—from companionship to advanced medical care—with complete flexibility to adjust as needs change.

Package 1

Companion Care

₹18,000 /month or ₹700/day

Perfect for active seniors who need daily assistance and companionship

What's Included

  • 12-hour daily shifts (day or night)
  • Help with daily activities (bathing, dressing, grooming)
  • Meal preparation and feeding assistance
  • Medication reminders (non-medical)
  • Light housekeeping
  • Companionship and conversation
  • Assistance with mobility around the home
  • Shopping and errand support
  • Basic vitals monitoring (temperature, pulse)
  • Daily activity reports via app

Ideal For

Seniors who are generally independent but need help with certain tasks
Families concerned about loneliness and isolation
Post-hospitalization recovery (non-medical)
When family members work during the day

Medical Monitoring

Professional medical monitoring for chronic conditions and health management

What's Included

  • All Companion Care features, plus:

  • Comprehensive vitals monitoring (BP, sugar, oxygen, temperature)
  • Medication administration and management
  • Diabetes care (insulin administration, diet tracking)
  • Hypertension management
  • Doctor visit coordination and accompaniment
  • Health record maintenance
  • Lab test coordination
  • Digital health reports for doctor consultations
  • Real-time health alerts via family app
  • Weekly supervisor health reviews

Ideal For

Managing chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, heart disease)
Seniors on multiple medications requiring precise administration
Families living in different cities wanting remote health monitoring
Post-hospitalization with ongoing medical needs

Advanced Care

Round-the-clock medical care for complex health needs

  • Catheter care and management
  • Tracheostomy care
  • Feeding tube assistance (NG tube, PEG tube)
  • Bedsore prevention and treatment
  • Oxygen therapy monitoring
  • Physiotherapy exercises (basic)

  • Night-time monitoring and care
  • Emergency response protocols
  • Bi-weekly supervisor assessments

Ideal For

Post-surgery recovery requiring medical procedures
Bedbound or severely limited mobility patients
Complex medical equipment requiring skilled monitoring
Seniors requiring constant supervision and care
Get Started with Advanced Care
Package 4

Specialized Care

₹38,000 /month Condition-specific expertise

Expert care for dementia, Parkinson's, stroke recovery, and palliative needs

What's Included

  • All Advanced Care features, plus:
  • Specialized training for specific conditions
  • Dementia/Alzheimer's Care: Memory care, behavioral management, safety monitoring
  • Parkinson's Care: Movement assistance, medication timing, fall prevention
  • Stroke Recovery: Rehabilitation support, speech therapy assistance, mobility training
  • Palliative Care: Pain management, comfort care, emotional support
  • Cognitive stimulation activities
  • Physical therapy exercises (advanced)
  • Nutritional management for specific conditions
  • Family counseling and support
  • Coordination with specialist doctors
  • Customized care protocols
  • Weekly specialist supervisor consultations

Ideal For

Dementia or Alzheimer's patients requiring specialized approach
Parkinson's disease with motor challenges
Post-stroke rehabilitation at home
End-of-life care with dignity and comfort
Get Started with Specialized Care

  • Health equipment rental coordination
